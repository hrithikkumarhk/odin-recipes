Pumpkin Cheesecake

Description

Scoopable pumpkin cheesecake delivers rich, balanced fall flavors in a thick, velvety filling—all without turning on the oven. Made in just three easy steps, this cozy no-bake, make-ahead dessert gets the perfect finishing crunch from a buttery graham cracker topping.

Ingredients

  • 1 (8-ounce) package cream cheese, softened
  • 1 (24.3-ounce) container cheesecake filling (such as Philadelphia)
  • 3/4 cup canned pumpkin puree
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 (3.4-ounce) package pumpkin spice instant pudding
  • 1/2 cup cold whole milk
  • 1 cup frozen whipped topping, thawed (such as Cool Whip)
  • 2 cups coarsely crushed graham crackers
  • 4 tablespoons melted salted butter
  • 1/4 cup caramel sauce

Steps

  1. Using an electric hand mixer, beat cream cheese on medium speed until smooth in a large bowl, about 2 minutes. Add cheesecake filling, pumpkin puree, and salt, and continue to beat on medium until well combined, about 2 minutes.
  2. Whisk together pudding mix and milk in a bowl until very thick. Transfer to cream cheese mixture; beat on medium speed until smooth, about 1 minute. Fold in whipped topping until well combined. Transfer to an 8-x-8-inch baking dish; spread in an even layer.
  3. Stir together graham crackers and melted butter until well coated. Sprinkle over top of the cheesecake filling in an even layer. Lightly cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 8 hours or overnight. Drizzle with caramel sauce before serving.
