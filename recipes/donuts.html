Banana Pudding Hawaiian Roll “Donuts”

Description

Banana pudding Hawaiian roll “donuts” are creamy and sweet, with the perfect vanilla-banana finish. The pudding filling takes center stage, while vanilla glaze, crushed wafers, and fresh bananas capture everything you love about the classic dessert.

Ingredients

  • 1 (12-count) package Hawaiian rolls, separated into 12 rolls
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 medium bananas, sliced and divided
  • 1 (3.5-ounce) package banana instant pudding mix
  • 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons whole milk, divided
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract, divided
  • 1/8 teaspoon plus 1 pinch kosher salt, divided
  • 1 1/2 cups whipped topping
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 cup crushed mini vanilla wafers, plus 12 whole vanilla wafers for garnish

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (180 degrees C). Brush rolls with melted butter on all sides and place on a large rimmed baking sheet
  2. Bake in the preheated oven until lightly browned and toasted, about 5 minutes.
  3. Mash about 1 cup of the banana slices in a large bowl until smooth. Whisk in pudding mix, 1 cup milk, 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract, and 1/8 teaspoon salt until well combined and thickened. Fold in whipped topping. Refrigerate for 15 to 20 minutes to set. Transfer filling to a piping bag or a plastic bag. Make a small cut in the corner if using a plastic bag.
  4. Use a paring knife to make a small cut into one side of each roll, creating an opening large enough to pipe filling into without going all the way through. Fill each hole with about 1 to 1 1/2 tablespoons of filling.
  5. Whisk together powdered sugar, remaining 2 tablespoons milk, 1 teaspoon vanilla, and 1 pinch salt in a bowl until smooth.
  6. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Dip the top of each filled roll in vanilla glaze and place on the prepared baking sheet. Sprinkle each with crushed vanilla wafers. Top with remaining sliced bananas and whole vanilla wafers.
  7. Let sit at room temperature until the glaze sets slightly, about 10 minutes, or refrigerate for 5 minutes before serving.
